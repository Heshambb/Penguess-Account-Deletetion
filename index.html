Account and Data Deletion

CG7 Technology LLC provides users of the Melt the Ice game the ability to request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated gameplay data, please email us at:

support@cg7technology.com

In your email, please include:

  • Your in-game username (if set)
  • Your device platform (Android)
  • A request stating that you want your account and associated data deleted

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Anonymous account identifier
  • Gameplay progress and history
  • In-game currency balance
  • Analytics data linked to the account (where applicable)

Data That May Be Retained

We may retain limited, aggregated, and anonymized data for legal compliance, fraud prevention, and analytics reporting. This data cannot be used to identify users.

Retention Period

Account and associated data will be deleted within 30 days of receiving a valid deletion request.
